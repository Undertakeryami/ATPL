Introduction!

It is good to look at the history of all the models, when After the Cathode ray experiment, which proved that there are some particles with very smaller mass, but they have negative charge, existed in this world, then many scientist started giving proposals for their model of atoms, and started discovery of it. JJ Thmopshon, thought that a atom is pudding like structure where the negative charges are like like the fruits in the pudding and the positive charges are like the pudding creame itslef, hence the pudding model was developed

But soon in 1911, Rutherford came with an experiment, where he showed that when he bombarded a thin gold foil with Helium particle (+2) aplha rays, the results were interesting, rather than interesting they were suprising He saw that majorlity of the alpha rays were gone straight through, and some deviated with positive angles, (which means there is something in the center small compared to the size of the atom, but its positive as it is deflecting the alpha (postive) rays), and also 1 in 10000 particles got reflected back at 180 degrees, which was also a huge finding, which confirms something heavy at the center of the nucleus resides. After this experiment, several models of atom came to existence, and hence now we are using the model which we love, which is Quantum orbital model
